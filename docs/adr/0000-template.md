# NNNN. Short title of the decision

- Status: proposed | accepted | rejected | deprecated | superseded by NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: GitHub handles of the people who made the decision

## Context

What is the problem, and what forces are at play (technical, product, legal, people)?
State facts, not the decision. Link to issues and discussions.

## Considered options

- Option 1
- Option 2

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two sentences, followed by the essential details.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what follow-up work this creates.
Include the negative consequences, not only the positive ones.
